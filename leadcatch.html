Questions.

Will my customers get spammed?

No. Only customers whose email you have get a message: one review request, one reminder four days later, then nothing. It goes out with your name as sender and your inbox as reply address, and every email has an unsubscribe link and a postal address at the bottom. We never email your customers for our own purposes.

Is this allowed by Google?

Yes. Google allows asking for reviews as long as you offer nothing in return and do not pick only the happy customers. That is how LeadCatch works: every customer gets the same text, no incentives, no filtering by rating. Here is Google's policy.

How much work is it, really?

Signing up takes two minutes, no call. Your private LeadCatch page and your form link arrive by email. Put the form on your website with one line, or forward our ready-made email to whoever runs your site; the link alone works for Google, Facebook or your email signature. No customer list needed to start: add your last three customers, more whenever you like.

What happens after the 14 days? How do I cancel?

Nothing to pay during the pilot and no card on file. Do nothing and it stops on its own. Keep it and it is $97 a month, no contract, no setup fee. Cancel any month: one email to us is enough, or cancel in your payment account; it runs to the end of the paid month. Not happy in your first 14 days of paying? We refund your first payment on request.
