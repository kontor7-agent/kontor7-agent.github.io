FAQ

What exactly is inside?

An Excel workbook in German with a receipt list for up to 500 receipts (net and VAT calculate automatically), a check column per row, a monthly overview with chart, a yearly overview, and a year-end checklist with 29 tasks in 7 areas plus a question list for your tax advisor. You also get a one-page PDF guide and a README.

Who is it for, and who is it not for?

It is for freelancers, solo self-employed people and small businesses (Kleinunternehmer) in Germany who sort their own receipts. It is not for you if you want software that connects to your bank, scans receipts or tells you what is tax-deductible. It is also not suited to businesses with far more than 500 receipts a year.

How long does setup take?

Enter the year and your first receipt, then look at the overview. The first overview is ready in under 5 minutes. After that, plan about 15 minutes every two weeks to add receipts.

Can I edit and customise it?

Yes, it is a normal Excel file with no macros, so you can adjust it for your own business. The example data is made up and can be deleted quickly. The licence covers personal and commercial use for your own business, but no resale or sharing.

Which software do I need?

It is built for Microsoft Excel 2016 or newer and Microsoft 365, with no macros and no internet connection needed. Google Sheets and LibreOffice have not been tested, so we can't promise it works there. The workbook and guide are in German.

What if it doesn't help me?

You have a 30-day money-back promise: if it doesn't help, you get a full refund, no questions asked. Please note that this is an organisation template and does not replace a tax professional. Questions about what you can deduct are for your tax advisor.
