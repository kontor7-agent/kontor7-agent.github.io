FAQ

What exactly is inside?

One Excel workbook with a Rate Calculator sheet, a one-page printable A4 Invoice sheet and a How to use sheet. A short README and a cover image are included too. The calculator turns your target income, overhead, tax set-aside %, time off, hours and billable share into a minimum and a recommended hourly and day rate.

Who is it for, and who is it not for?

It is for new and early-stage freelancers who are unsure what to charge and want a clear starting number plus a clean invoice. It is not for you if you need full accounting, time tracking or automatic payment reminders. It is also not a replacement for a tax professional, accountant or lawyer.

How long does setup take?

There is nothing to install and no macros. You open the file, fill the yellow cells on 'Rate Calculator' and read your rates. Then you edit the 'Invoice' sheet with your details. How long that takes depends on how quickly you can pull together your income goal and costs.

Can I edit and customise it?

Yes. It is a normal Excel file, so you can change the invoice wording, your details and the tax/VAT rate (you enter it yourself). The due date is calculated as invoice date plus your payment terms, and line totals and subtotal update automatically.

Which software do I need?

It is built for Excel 2013 or newer and tested in LibreOffice Calc. The listing also names Google Sheets as a supported option, but we have not tested it as thoroughly, so check your invoice layout and print preview first. We do not claim Apple Numbers support.
