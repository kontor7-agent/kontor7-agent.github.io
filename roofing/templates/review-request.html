The Google review request e-mail, plus the reminder

The exact two e-mails: one request, one reminder. Copy, fill in four fields, send.

E-MAIL 1 - send within one or two days of the final walk-through

Subject: Quick favor, [First name]?

Hi [First name],

thanks again for trusting us with the roof on [Street]. It was a pleasure working for you.

If you have two minutes, a short Google review helps the next homeowner find us. Here is the direct link:
[Review link]

Two or three honest sentences are perfect. Thank you.

[Your name]
[Company] - [Phone]


E-MAIL 2 - the reminder, 4 to 7 days later, same thread

Subject: Re: Quick favor, [First name]?

Hi [First name],

just a gentle nudge in case this slipped through. Here is the link again:
[Review link]

If you already left one: thank you, and please ignore this.

[Your name]


THE RULES (Google's and ours)
- Same text to every customer. No picking the happy ones.
- Never ask for stars, never offer a discount, never write the review for them.
- One request, one reminder, then stop.
- Your review link: Google Business Profile > Ask for reviews > copy link.
